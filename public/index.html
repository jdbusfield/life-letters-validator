Ways to Find Your Photos

  • Start with your camera roll. Find a person, pet, place, or object that matches your word.
  • Search your smartphone’s photos. On your smartphone, you can search for words or locations to find photos from your life more easily!
  • Search for a picture. Try Google Images and take a screenshot of the picture you choose.
  • Take the photos yourself. Have a photo shoot with your child holding the objects or doing the activity that matches the word.

Your photos stay private

We use your photos and personalization details to create, print, and support your Life Letters order. They are shared only with our team and the service providers needed to fulfill it.

Your personalization files in the survey are scheduled for deletion 90 days after your finished cards ship to you, unless they are still needed to resolve an order issue or meet a legal requirement.

We don’t use your photos in advertising or social media without separate permission. For questions or deletion requests, email contact@life-letters.com.

Printing, working copies, and backups

Printing requires sharing your personalized PDF with Canva. Our team separately checks removal of Canva uploads and downloaded working copies. Service-provider backups may remain for additional periods; this notice does not promise that every backup copy is erased on day 90.
